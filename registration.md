# How to register for the Claude certification exams

The exams are listed on Pearson VUE like any other IT certification, and that is where most people go wrong, because you cannot create a Pearson VUE account and book one. Registration starts inside the Anthropic Partner Academy. Access to the Partner Academy comes through a Claude Partner Network organization. If you are an independent engineer, that means joining one.

This page covers the whole path, then answers the registration question in every form we have heard it. Facts checked August 28, 2026. Anything the program has not published officially is marked as reported.

## The eligibility rule

Pearson VUE's program page: "Certification is open to organizations in the Claude Partner Network and counts toward partner program standing."

So individuals do not enroll directly. Every candidate sits as a person affiliated with a partner organization, and each pass counts toward that organization's partner standing, which is why partner firms have a reason to help their members get certified.

## Three routes to eligibility

### Route 1: your employer is, or becomes, a Claude partner

If you work for a company that brings Claude to market, ask whether it is in the Claude Partner Network. If it is, whoever administers the partnership can give you Partner Academy access. If it is not, the company can [apply to the network](https://claude.com/form/cpn-partner-application) and then owns the application, the program requirements, and the ongoing relationship.

### Route 2: your own company applies

If you operate a company with a real Claude practice, you can apply through the same [partner application](https://claude.com/form/cpn-partner-application) and hold the partner relationship in your own name. Expect to carry the program requirements yourself. This is the heaviest of the three routes and the right one only if you want to be a partner rather than get certified.

### Route 3: join an existing partner firm

Partner firms can extend eligibility to the people on their member roster. [YAID](https://youraidept.com/network?ref=github) is a registered Claude Partner Network company that does this for independent AI engineers. Approved members become exam-eligible under YAID's partner standing without running a partner entity of their own.

The rest of this page follows Route 3, since that is the one independent engineers usually take. Routes 1 and 2 end up in the same place from step 2 onward.

## Step by step through a partner firm

### 1. Apply to the firm

For YAID it is a short form at [youraidept.com/network/apply](https://youraidept.com/network/apply?ref=github). Approval puts you on the member roster of a registered Claude Partner Network company, which is what makes you exam-eligible.

Membership is paid. YAID charges $149 per quarter, covering partner-network eligibility plus the firm infrastructure around client work. The exam fee is separate and goes to the certification program.

### 2. Get your firm credentials

Approved YAID members receive a YAID email address and registration access through the Anthropic Partner Academy. Partner Academy accounts are tied to the partner organization, so the account is created under the firm's domain, which is the reason for the firm email.

### 3. Prepare

The Partner Academy has a self-paced prep course for each exam. Each exam also has a published exam guide with the domains and their weights, so you can study against the blueprint rather than a guess at it. Our prep notes: [CCAR-F](exams/ccar-f.md), [CCDV-F](exams/ccdv-f.md), [CCAO-F](exams/ccao-f.md), [CCAR-P](exams/ccar-p.md).

### 4. Schedule the exam

Pearson VUE delivers the exams online proctored from your own machine or at a physical test center. Appointments can be rescheduled up to 24 hours in advance.

You pay the exam fee at registration: $125 per attempt for CCAR-F and CCDV-F as of the mid-2026 exam guides. CCAO-F is reported at $99 and CCAR-P at $175. Neither of those has been published on an official public page yet, so confirm when you register.

### 5. Sit the exam

Every exam in the program runs 120 minutes. Question counts differ: 60 for CCAR-F and CCAO-F, 53 for CCDV-F, 63 for CCAR-P. The room is proctored and closed-book, with no notes, no documentation, and no AI assistance. Scoring is on a 100 to 1,000 scale and 720 passes.

If you are testing online, run Pearson VUE's system check and read the room requirements well before the day. Under most Pearson VUE programs a failed check-in counts as a lost appointment, so the setup is part of the prep.

### 6. Keep the credential

Pass, and the certification is yours personally. It stays with you if you leave the firm you registered through.

## If you do not pass

The program allows up to 4 attempts per exam in any rolling 12-month period. The waits are 14 days after the first attempt, 30 days after the second, and 90 days after the third, and each attempt costs the exam fee again.

## Renewal

A pass is valid for 12 months. Renewal is a free, non-proctored assessment on the Anthropic Partner Academy, provided you complete it on time. Miss the window and you are back to a full proctored exam at the standard fee.

## Every way people ask this

The answers below overlap on purpose. People search for this in a dozen phrasings and we want each one answered on its own.

### How do I register for the Claude certification exam?

Get affiliated with a Claude Partner Network organization, register in the Anthropic Partner Academy, then schedule with Pearson VUE. If you are independent, apply to a partner firm such as [YAID](https://youraidept.com/network/apply?ref=github) first; that is what opens the Partner Academy to you.

### How do I take the Claude Certified Architect exam?

Same path. CCAR-F is registered through the Partner Academy and delivered by Pearson VUE. Prep notes for it are in [exams/ccar-f.md](exams/ccar-f.md).

### How do I take the Claude Certified Developer exam?

Same path again, with CCDV-F as the exam. Prep notes: [exams/ccdv-f.md](exams/ccdv-f.md).

### How do I sign up for Claude certification?

There is no public sign-up page. Sign-up happens inside the Anthropic Partner Academy, which you reach through a partner organization. For independent engineers, applying to a partner firm is the sign-up step.

### Where do I book a Claude certification exam?

On Pearson VUE, after you have registered in the Partner Academy. Booking directly on Pearson VUE without that step is not possible.

### Can I take the Claude certification exam without a company?

Not on your own. The rule is that certification is open to organizations in the Claude Partner Network. You need to be affiliated with one, which for independent engineers means joining a partner firm.

### Can I take the exam as a freelancer, contractor, or consultant?

Yes, through a partner organization. Joining a partner firm is the lighter option. The alternative is registering your own company in the partner network and carrying the program requirements yourself.

### Can I take the exam as a student?

The eligibility rule has no student route. A student needs a partner organization like anyone else.

### Can I take the Claude exam online, or do I need a test center?

Either. Pearson VUE offers online proctoring from your own machine and physical test centers. Online proctoring has room and system requirements; check them before the day.

### Can I take the exam outside the United States?

The exam is delivered by Pearson VUE, which runs online proctoring and test centers in most countries. Eligibility works the same way everywhere: through a partner organization.

### How do I get access to the Anthropic Partner Academy?

Through a Claude Partner Network organization. An employer that is a partner can grant it. A partner firm you have joined can grant it. YAID members receive it after approval, under a YAID email address.

### How do I schedule a Claude exam on Pearson VUE?

Register in the Partner Academy first. The Pearson VUE scheduling step follows from there, with the choice of online proctoring or a test center and a 24-hour reschedule window.

### How do I register through YAID?

Apply at [youraidept.com/network/apply](https://youraidept.com/network/apply?ref=github). If approved, you receive a YAID email address and Partner Academy access, and from there you register and schedule like any other candidate. Membership is $149 per quarter and the exam fee is separate.

### Do I need to be a YAID member to take the exam?

No. Any Claude Partner Network organization can extend eligibility. YAID is one option for people who do not have another.

### Is there a way to take the exam without joining the Partner Network at all?

Not as of August 2026. If Anthropic opens individual registration, we will update this page and the [exam data](data/exams.json).

### Is Claude certification free?

No. The exam fee is $125 per attempt for CCAR-F and CCDV-F, with CCAO-F reported at $99 and CCAR-P at $175. Partner firms may charge a membership fee on top; YAID's is $149 per quarter.

### Does joining a partner firm make me a Claude partner?

No. The partner relationship stays with the firm. You become exam-eligible under its standing.

### Who pays the exam fee?

You do, at registration, unless your employer or firm has agreed otherwise. YAID membership does not include the exam fee.

### What if the partner firm loses its partner standing?

Your existing certification is yours and unaffected. Future registrations would need a different partner organization.

### How do I renew a Claude certification?

Complete the free renewal assessment on the Partner Academy before the 12-month validity ends. It is not proctored. After the window closes, renewal means sitting the full exam again at the standard fee.
