# Contributing

Corrections and additions are welcome. This repository exists to be the accurate, sourced reference for how Claude certification works, so a change to a fact needs a source behind it.

## What we accept

- Fact corrections with a source. Exam fees, question counts, domain weights, retake rules, and validity periods change. If something here is out of date, open a pull request that cites the current exam guide, the Pearson VUE program page, or an Anthropic announcement.
- Prep notes and study approaches. What you studied, how you weighted the domains, what surprised you. Keep it general enough that it does not describe a specific exam question.
- Community resources. Study guides, practice exams, labs, and courses that are free or clearly priced. Add them to the README list with a one-line description.
- Registration path details. If you registered through a route other than a partner firm, notes on how it went will help the next person.

## What we close

- Exam questions. Do not post questions you saw on an exam, paraphrased or otherwise. Every candidate agrees to Pearson VUE's and Anthropic's exam conduct terms, and reproducing exam content violates them.
- Changes to facts without a source.
- Promotional edits. Links to paid courses or services are fine when they are useful and labeled as paid. Rewriting the guide around a product is not.

## How to contribute

1. Fork the repository and make your change on a branch.
2. If you change a fact, update the "facts checked" date and add the source.
3. If you change exam facts, update both the markdown page and [`data/exams.json`](data/exams.json) so they stay in sync.
4. Open a pull request with a short note on what changed and why.

## Style

Plain prose. Say what is verified and what is reported. Content is licensed under CC BY 4.0, so only contribute material you have the right to license that way.
